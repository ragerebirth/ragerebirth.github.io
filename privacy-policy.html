This Privacy Policy explains why and how RageRebirth ("we," "us," or "our") processes information when you install and play our Android mobile games (the "Apps & Games") available on Google Play. It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Our Apps & Games are not intended for users under 16. If you are under 16, please do not use them.

01 Data We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers or databases that store your personal information. We do not collect your name, postal address, phone number, or email address.

However, when you play our Apps & Games, certain technical information is processed automatically by the third-party services built into them (see section 03). Depending on the game and your privacy choices, this may include:

  • Identifiers — your device's Advertising ID (GAID), app-instance identifiers, and IP address.
  • Advertising and interaction data — which ads were shown or tapped, and ad performance and fraud-detection signals.
  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type, and performance or diagnostic information.
  • Approximate location — coarse, country- or region-level location inferred from your IP address. We do not access your precise GPS location.
  • Gameplay and usage data — session activity and in-game events, to the extent a third-party service records them, including analytics and install-attribution services such as GameAnalytics, Facebook Analytics, and Adjust.
  • Purchase information — if you make an in-app purchase, Google Play processes the transaction. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.
  • Google Play Games data — if you use Play Games features (leaderboards, achievements, sign-in), Google handles your profile and game data under your Google account.

Game progress that is saved only on your device stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

03 Who Receives Data

Because we do not operate our own back end, data is not shared by us from a database of ours. Instead, it is collected by, and shared with, the following third-party services built into the Apps & Games. Where they decide how your data is used, they act as independent controllers under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob and its advertising and mediation partners — display advertisements and measure ad performance. They may process identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device information, and app usage and interaction data, including for personalized ads where permitted and consented. See Google's Ads Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases entirely within Google Play. We only learn whether a purchase was completed, not your payment details. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services — provides leaderboards, achievements, and sign-in through your Google Play Games account. We do not manage or store this data ourselves. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GameAnalytics — analytics service that records how players use our games, so we can understand and improve them. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy.
  • Facebook Analytics — analytics service used to understand how players interact with our games. See Facebook's privacy policy.
  • Adjust — analytics and attribution service that measures how players find and use our games. See Adjust's privacy policy.
  • Voodoo — provides an SDK package that retrieves the data collected by the analytics partners listed above. Voodoo does not itself collect your personal data. See section 10 for contact details.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities — we may disclose information where required to comply with a legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. We do not share your information with anyone except as described in this policy.

05 Children's Privacy

Our Apps & Games are not designed for or directed at children under 16. If you are under 16, do not use them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow users under 16 to be shown personalized advertising without any consent required by law.

Age ratings assigned by Google Play reflect that platform's content classification and do not mean the Apps & Games are intended for children. Minors between 16 and 18 are encouraged to review this policy with a parent or trusted adult.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through a game, contact us at the address in section 14 and we will help you resolve it, including by directing you to the relevant third party to request deletion.

06 International Transfers

The Apps & Games are available worldwide. Data processed by Google and its partners may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Those providers rely on safeguards such as adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses, or certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as described in their own policies.

07 Your Rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Swiss law, to the extent we or our partners hold data about you:

  • Access and portability — request a copy of your data.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure — request deletion, unless retention is required by law. See section 10 for how.
  • Restriction — request that processing be limited in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing relies on consent, through the consent prompt or ad settings described in section 09 or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country of residence.

Since most of the data above is held by Google and our other partners as independent controllers, requests about that data are best made to them directly. For Google, use your Google Account and Ads Settings; for our other partners, see the contacts in section 10. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help point your request to the right place.

08 Your Rights in the United States

Residents of U.S. states with consumer privacy laws (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, and others) may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Know and access the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, and how it is used and disclosed.
  • Delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions. See section 10 for how.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. However, allowing advertising partners to collect identifiers and device information through the ad SDK may count as a "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out through your device's ad settings (see section 09) or by emailing us.
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Appeal a denied request by replying to our response or emailing us with "Privacy Appeal" in the subject line.

Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (Advertising ID, IP address); internet or other electronic network activity (ad interaction, in-game event, and app usage data); and coarse location inferred from your IP address. We do not collect sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit its use. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

You may use an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf; we may need to verify the agent's authority. California residents may also request information about disclosures to third parties for their direct marketing purposes — we make none.

09 Your Choices

  • Consent prompt — where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the Apps & Games ask for your consent to personalized advertising before ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the game provides a privacy settings option, or by reinstalling and responding to the prompt again.
  • Android advertising controls — go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to opt out of ads personalization or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Google Ads Settings — manage personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • Google account data — manage or delete Google Play Games and account data through your Google Account settings.
  • Uninstall — removing a game from your device stops all further collection by that game.
