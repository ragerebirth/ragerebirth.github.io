This Privacy Policy describes how RageRebirth ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile game(s) (the "App"), available on Google Play. The App is intended for a general, worldwide audience and can be downloaded and played by anyone, in any country.

01 Data We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal data through the App. We have not built any account system, server, or database of our own. Any data collection that occurs is performed by third-party services integrated into the App, as described below.

02 Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may independently collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements within the App. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, general location, and app usage data to serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Learn more at Google's Ads Policy and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases) — purchases made in the App are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information; this is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Games Services — features such as leaderboards, achievements, and sign-in are handled automatically through your existing Google Play Games account. We do not manage, store, or have access to this data ourselves; it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

03 Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at collecting personal information from children. Where the App is made available to a general audience, any ads served may be configured (via Google Play Console / AdMob settings) to comply with applicable child-directed treatment requirements, such as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy, as configured by the developer in the Google Play Console. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone directly, regardless of age.

04 Your Choices

You can control ad personalization and data collection related to advertising through your device's ad settings (e.g., Google Settings → Ads, or Android Settings → Privacy → Ads) and through Google's own Ads Settings page. Any data related to your Google Play Games or Google account is managed through your Google Account settings.

05 Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, we hold no user data that could be exposed. Any data handled by AdMob, Google Play Billing, or Google Play Games Services is secured according to Google's own security practices.

06 International Use

The App is available to users worldwide. By using the App, you acknowledge that any data processed by the third-party services above may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, in accordance with those providers' own policies.
