The Premise

Arcade reflexes. RPG depth.

Rage Rebirth reimagines the classic brick-breaker inside a dark, living dungeon. Every wall you shatter is a room in a cave system that fights back — guarded by traps, side quests, and secrets that reward exploration as much as reflexes. Swap between soldiers mid-run, chain special attacks, and chase the Super Orb hidden at the heart of each level.
